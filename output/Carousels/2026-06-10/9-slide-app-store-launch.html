05 · Insight 4
App Store Insights
04
Approved
≠
Searchable.
After approval, expect 2–3 days before your app shows up in search. Apple is still indexing your metadata and copyright info.
Don't panic. It's coming.

Push an update in your first few weeks and the indexing clock starts over. You may temporarily vanish from search again.
Plan your update timing.
