Slide 3 of 10 — The One Constant
PLACES.
THE ONE CONSTANT
WHAT SURVIVED EVERY PIVOT
Saving places so users could own their memories, not just their photos.
WHY IT MATTERS
Every version changed. That didn't. The idea that someone should be able to save a place, revisit it, and feel confident it's theirs — that was always the foundation.
@yoobinseo
Slide 4 of 10 — Building on Assumptions
ASSUME.
HOW WE BUILT
THE METHOD
We built on assumptions. Not blind ones — but still assumptions.
THE PROBLEM
We observed people. Noticed behaviors. Watched how people traveled. We thought that was enough to build on.
@yoobinseo
Slide 5 of 10 — The Missing Piece
ASK.
WHAT WAS MISSING
THE GAP
We never talked to a single potential user.
WHAT THAT MEANS
No outreach. No discovery. No "does this solve your problem?" We built for people we never actually found — and never confirmed needed what we were making.
@yoobinseo
Slide 6 of 10 — Observing vs Talking
LISTEN.
THE REALIZATION
WHAT'S DIFFERENT
Observing people and talking to them are completely different things.
THE DISTINCTION
Observations tell you what people do. Conversations tell you why — and whether they'd actually change their behavior to fix it.
@yoobinseo
Slide 7 of 10 — What It Cost
COST.
WHAT IT COST
THE HONEST PART
We built confidently in the wrong direction. For years.
WHY
Not because the idea was bad. Because we never stress-tested it against the people it was actually built for.
@yoobinseo
Slide 8 of 10 — The Lesson
TRUTH.
THE LESSON
WHAT I KNOW NOW
The product tells you what it wants to be.
THE PART WE MISSED
But only if you let real people tell it. That's the step we skipped. Don't skip it.
@yoobinseo
Slide 9 of 10 — What I Do Now
VALIDATE.
WHAT I DO NOW
THE RULE
Find real people before you build anything.
THE STANDARD
Not 3 people who say "cool idea." 3 people who say "when can I have it" — and can tell you exactly what problem it's solving for them.
