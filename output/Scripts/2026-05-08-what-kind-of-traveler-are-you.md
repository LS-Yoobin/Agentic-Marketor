# "What Kind of Traveler Are You?" — Production Script

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (TikTok / Instagram Reels)
**Length:** ~28–32 seconds
**Account:** Yoobin personal brand
**Date:** 2026-05-08

---

## Shot-by-Shot

### INTRO (2s)
**Text card:** `What kind of traveler are you?`
Fast cut into types.

---

### TYPE 1: THE OVERPACKER (3s)
**Shot:** Dragging 4 massive suitcases up just 2 stairs. One falls over mid-drag.
**Text overlay:** `packs for war`
**Sub-text:** `3 day trip btw.`

---

### TYPE 2: THE OVER-PLANNER (3s)
**Shot:** Holding a massive binder. Google Maps open on phone. Color-coded spreadsheets spread everywhere. Looks completely sleep deprived.
**Text overlay:** `planned every minute`
**Gag:** Still misses the flight.

---

### TYPE 3: THE CONTENT CREATOR (3s)
**Shot:** Holding phone up everywhere — records the food, records walking, records clouds. Then walks directly into a pole/wall.
**Text overlay:** `forgot to enjoy the trip`

---

### TYPE 4: THE RAWDOGGER (3s)
**Shot:** At the airport. Shrug. No hotel. No itinerary. Just standing there. Just vibes.
**Text overlay:** `figures it out later`

---

### TYPE 5: THE CAMERA ROLL HOARDER (4s)
**Shot:** 47 nearly identical sunset photos on screen. "Storage Full" notification covering the display. Phone shows 10,000+ unread photos.
**Text overlay:** `never looks at them again`

---

### TYPE 6: THE BLOGGO USER (5–6s)
**Shot:** Music changes — everything slows down and goes calm. Phone screen shows travel photos automatically organizing into a beautiful blog.
**Text overlay 1:** `your photos already contain the story.`
**Text overlay 2:** `Tap to Blog.`

## Props Checklist
- [ ] 4 suitcases/bags (Overpacker)
- [ ] Binder + printed papers/spreadsheets (Over-Planner)
- [ ] Phone + something to walk into (Content Creator)
- [ ] Boarding pass prop, optional (Rawdogger)
- [ ] "Storage Full" overlay — add in edit (Camera Roll Hoarder)
- [ ] Phone screen recording of Bloggo generating a blog (Bloggo User)

---

## Music Direction
- Types 1–5: Fast, punchy, slightly chaotic beat
- Type 6: Hard stop → calm/cinematic transition

---

## Caption
`Which one are you? 👇`

## Self-Duplication Tip
Best used for **The Overpacker** — split-screen yourself carrying luggage from both sides. Highest visual payoff for solo filming.
