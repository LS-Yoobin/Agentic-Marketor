## DAY 1 — Hot Take Reel (30–45 sec talking head)

### Script

**[Beat 1 — Hook, lean slightly forward, drop it flat]**
"AI didn't make building apps easier. It made excuses harder."

**[Beat 2 — Pull back slightly, let the idea breathe, direct and even]**
"Everyone talks about what AI removed. No CS degree. No big team. No $500k to hire engineers. And yeah — all of that is true. The barriers are lower than they've ever been."

**[Beat 3 — Shift tone. Slower. More pointed.]**
"But here's what nobody says out loud: if the barriers are gone and you still haven't built anything — that's information. There's no engineer to blame. No budget to hide behind. No 'I would, but...' that actually holds up anymore."

**[Beat 4 — Payoff. Straight into camera. Confident, not mean.]**
"AI doesn't help builders. It exposes who was never willing to build in the first place. If you're building something right now — keep going. The bar for shipping just got lower. Use it."

---

### Filming Notes

- **Camera distance:** Medium close-up — head and shoulders, slight chest. Not full upper body.
- **Framing:** Centered. Eye-level. No tilt.
- **Environment:** Clean background — a wall, a window, wherever you actually work. No setups for the sake of it.
- **Delivery:** Beat 1 is flat and direct — no rise in energy, just confident. Beat 2 can be conversational. Beat 3 slows down. Beat 4 is the only beat where you hold a half-second pause before the last sentence.
- **Energy level:** 6/10. This is not a hype post. It's a take. Measured > excited.
- **B-roll option:** None needed. Stay on face the whole time. The script carries it.

## DAY 3 — BTS / Low Energy Reel (15–30 sec, phone-held, unfiltered)

### Concept Options

---

**Option A — Code / Feature (screen recording or over-shoulder)**

**What to film:** Film over your own shoulder at your laptop screen while something is compiling, loading, or rendering. Alternatively, do a quick screen recording of a feature working for the first time — trip detection running, a blog generating, whatever is actually live that day.

**What to say (pick one or say nothing):**
- "This took longer than it should have. But it works now."
- Just the ambient typing/clicking sounds, no voice. Let it run 10–15 seconds. On-screen text: "still figuring this out."
- Or one line at the end: "Small win. Moving on."

**Length:** 10–20 seconds.

---

**Option B — Mindset / The Messy Middle (process, no polish)**

**What to film:** Your actual desk, notebook, open tabs. Film your hands writing something — a list, a decision you're working through, a question you don't have the answer to yet. Not staged. Ugly is fine.

**What to say:**
- Nothing. On-screen text only: "The part they don't show you."
- Or speak quietly, close to camera: "There's a version of this where I have it all figured out. This is not that version."

**Length:** 15–20 seconds.

---

**Option C — Small Human Moment (a quiet founder moment)**

**What to film:** Coffee cooling on the desk. Walking to get water. Staring at your phone screen in the dark. The moment before you get back to work. Something real and small.

**What to say:** One line max, or nothing.
- "Nobody tells you how much of building is just... sitting with uncertainty."
- Or just silence. Let the shot be the post.

**Length:** 10–15 seconds. Shorter is better here.

---

### Universal Caption (use for any option)

Not every day is a launch or a milestone.

Some days it's just the work. Quiet, a little messy, kind of uncertain.

Building Bloggo in public means I'm sharing those days too.

### On-Screen Text Beats

| Beat | Text | Hold Duration | Notes |
|------|------|---------------|-------|
| 1 | **POV: You hired Claude as your co-founder** | 2.5 sec | Title card — white text on black or dark overlay |
| 2 | **It doesn't sleep** | 2 sec | Cut to: timestamp on a laptop screen showing 1:47am or 2am — your actual late-night session |
| 3 | **It doesn't argue about the roadmap** | 2.5 sec | Cut to: clean Notion board or a merged PR — something tidy, something done |
| 4 | **It reviews its own code** | 2 sec | Cut to: Claude conversation screenshot — it catching a bug or flagging an issue in your code |
| 5 | **But it still needs you to know what you're building** | 3.5 sec | Hold longer. No cut. Let the screen sit. This is the point. |

**Total runtime:** ~14–15 seconds without VO, ~20–22 seconds with VO.

---

### Optional VO Script

Record over the final beat (beat 5) or as a single line at the end:

> "The tools are insane right now. The question is whether you know what to build with them."

Delivery: calm, not hyped. Like you're saying it to yourself as much as anyone else.

---

### Filming / Editing Notes

- **Beat 1:** Static title card. Dark background. Clean font. 0 animation.
- **Beat 2 (timestamp):** Real screengrab from a late session works. Or film your laptop screen at night — the glow in a dark room is enough.
- **Beat 3 (roadmap):** Use your actual Notion. Doesn't need to be pretty — real is better than staged.
- **Beat 4 (code review):** Screenshot of a real Claude conversation where it caught something. Blur anything sensitive. Crop to the relevant exchange.
- **Beat 5:** Hold on the text. Do not cut away. If you're using VO, let the voice land before the video ends.
- **Transitions:** Hard cuts only. No dissolves. The pace is the point.
- **Music:** lo-fi or something with a beat drop before beat 5 works well. Keep it low in the mix if using VO.

## DAY 7 — Origin Story Talking Head (60–90 sec, sit-down, direct to camera)

### Script

**[Beat 1 — Open honest. Warm, not rehearsed. Look directly at camera.]**
"This account is new. I've been sitting on sharing this story for a while because I wasn't sure I was ready for people to actually watch. But I'm building something I care about — so I might as well be real about it from the beginning."

**[Beat 2 — The moment. Slow down. Specific. Let the memory be real.]**
"A couple of years ago I went on a trip. I think about it as one of the best I've ever taken — the food, the neighborhood, the way a particular evening felt. I took probably 800 photos. And six months later, someone asked me the name of the restaurant I'd talked about for weeks after I came home. I couldn't remember it. I went through my camera roll looking for something — a sign, a receipt, anything. Nothing. The photos were there but the story wasn't."

**[Pause. Let it land for half a second.]**

"That moment stuck with me in a way I didn't expect."

**[Beat 3 — The realization. Pick up the pace slightly — this is the connective tissue.]**
"Then I started asking people — friends, people I followed, strangers. 'What do you do with your trip photos?' And basically everyone said the same thing: nothing. They're in the camera roll. There's a loose plan to do something with them. That plan never happens. The memories fade and the photos just sit there."

**[Beat 4 — The building part. State it simply. Not a pitch — a fact.]**
"That's why Bloggo exists. It's an iOS app that takes your travel photos and turns them into a real blog — narratives, captions, maps, everything. All on your phone. No writing. No organizing. You tap a button and it's done. We're in the final stretch before launch."

**[Beat 5 — Why building in public. Honest. Slightly vulnerable, but grounded.]**
"I'm building in public because I genuinely don't have all the answers yet. I don't know if the launch goes perfectly. I don't know which of the decisions I'm making right now are the right ones. But I'd rather figure it out openly than disappear into a build cave and reappear when it's 'ready.'"

**[Beat 6 — CTA. Warm, direct, no performance.]**
"If you've ever come home from a trip with a full camera roll and nothing to show for it — I think you'll care about this. Follow along. I'll show you everything."

---

### Filming Notes

- **Environment:** Wherever you actually spend time — your desk, a couch, a coffee shop you like. No background staging. Real works better than setup here.
- **Framing:** Slightly wider than the Day 1 reel — mid-chest to top of head. This is a longer format so give the frame a little more room.
- **Lighting:** Natural window light if possible. Soft and even. This is not a polished interview — it should feel like a conversation, not a production.
- **Eye contact:** Direct to camera throughout. Break it very briefly during beat 2 when you're recalling the memory — looking slightly off-camera for a second feels real and is okay here.
- **Energy:** 5/10. This is the most intimate post of the week. No performance energy. You're just talking to someone.
- **One take vs. multiple:** Try to do it in 1–2 takes. The slight imperfections — a brief pause, a word you correct — make it land better. Don't over-edit this one.
- **Caption on screen:** Optional lower-third at the start with your name + "co-founder, Bloggo" if this is early enough in your account growth that not everyone knows who you are yet.
