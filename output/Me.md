## Current Projects

### Bloggo
**AI-powered iOS travel blog app.**
Turns a user's existing photo library into a story-driven travel blog in under 60 seconds — no writing, no organizing, no blank page. On-device AI only. Photos never leave the phone.
- My role: Co-founder — product, design, strategy, marketing
- Status: Built, in App Store review process
- Stack: iOS (Swift), on-device LLM, Apple Vision framework

### Covela
**AI-powered memory app for couples.**
A branch off Bloggo's core — same foundation of memory preservation, rebuilt for relationships. Couples capture shared moments, and Covela turns them into something lasting. More playful features than Bloggo, designed to strengthen the relationship, not just document it. The goal: give couples a reason to look back together.
- My role: Co-founder — product, design, strategy
- Status: In development
- Core insight: Bloggo preserves travel memories. Covela preserves *us*.

### Paper Swing Trader
**Semi-autonomous swing trading bot for US equities.**
Built in Python with a modular pipeline architecture. Scans 300–500 liquid stocks (S&P 500 + Russell 1000), generates momentum and mean-reversion signals, and sends trade candidates to Telegram for human approval before execution. Claude acts as a parallel risk annotator — never the decision-maker. Stop-losses execute automatically; entries always require my approval.
- My role: Builder / sole developer
- Status: In development (paper trading first, live after 30-day graduation threshold)
- Stack: Python, Alpaca Markets, Telegram bot, local dashboard
- Philosophy: Deterministic logic generates the alpha. I stay in the loop for every entry.

### Jarvis
**Personal AI voice assistant.**
A custom voice interface I'm building for my own productivity — talks to me, triggers workflows, connects tools. Built in Python, lives in this repo.
- My role: Builder / sole developer
- Status: Active development

## How I Think

**Ship before ready.**
The product is never ready. Shipping is how you get ready.

**Distribution is the job.**
Nobody is coming to find your product. You are the distribution, every single day.

**Obsession over motivation.**
Motivation fades. Discipline fades. The why doesn't. I write it down.

**Perspective is work.**
You can't see clearly from inside the problem. Stepping back isn't falling behind — it's the job.

**The idea is 5%.**
The rest is obsession, showing up, and refusing to quit when you should have.
