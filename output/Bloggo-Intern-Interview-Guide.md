# Bloggo — Intern Interview Guide
**Goal:** Evaluate English speaking ability, app experience, technical thinking, and team chemistry.
**Candidates:** Non-native English speakers (likely Korean students)
**App focus:** Bloggo — AI travel blog app

---

## Before You Start

- Let them speak freely first. Many questions will answer themselves naturally.
- Don't rush silences — non-native speakers need a moment to translate their thoughts.
- Listen for clarity and confidence, not perfect grammar.
- Keep energy warm and conversational. This is a chemistry check too.

---

## 1. Icebreaker (2–3 min)

*Goal: Ease them in, hear their natural English voice.*

- Tell me a little about yourself — where you're from, what you study, what you enjoy.
- Have you ever kept a travel journal or blog before? Even just photos on Instagram?

---

## 2. First Impressions of Bloggo (5 min)

*Goal: Onboarding clarity, initial reaction, overall mental model.*

- When you first opened Bloggo, what did you think it was for?
- Walk me through what happened the first time you used it. What did you tap first?
- Was anything confusing or unclear at the start?
- How would you describe Bloggo to a friend — in one or two sentences?

> **Note:** Let them describe it in their own words before jumping in. This reveals how well the value prop landed.

---

## 3. Core Experience & Feature Feedback (8–10 min)

*Goal: Detailed product feedback — what worked, what didn't.*

**Auto-Blog Generation**
- When Bloggo created your first blog, what was your reaction?
- How accurate were the trips it detected? Did it find the right places?
- How did the AI-written descriptions feel — did they capture the trip well, or did they feel off?

**Photos & Organization**
- Did the app pick the right photos, or did you have to remove a lot?
- How did the day-by-day and place-by-place grouping feel to you?

**The Blog Itself**
- Did you edit anything after the blog was created — titles, captions, places?
- What did the final blog look like? Did it feel like something you'd actually share?

**Export & Sharing**
- Did you try exporting as a PDF or video? How did that go?
- Did you share your blog with anyone? What was their reaction?

**In-App Camera (if they used it)**
- Did you try the in-app camera while traveling or on the go?
- Did you notice the Vibe feature — the ambient sound recording? What did you think?

---

## 4. Bugs, Crashes & Pain Points (5 min)

*Goal: Honest technical feedback.*

- Did you run into any bugs or moments where the app froze or crashed?
- Was there anything that felt slow — like processing, loading, or generating the blog?
- Was there a moment where you got stuck and didn't know what to do?
- Is there anything you expected the app to do that it didn't?

> **Tip:** If they hesitate, prompt with: *"Even small things count — anything that felt slightly off."*

---

## 5. Ideas & Creative Thinking (5 min)

*Goal: Technical imagination, product instinct.*

- If you could add one feature to Bloggo, what would it be?
- Is there an app you use daily that does something Bloggo could learn from?
- Bloggo currently works with travel photos — do you think it could work for other types of memories? Like food, events, daily life?
- What do you think would make someone open Bloggo every day, not just after a trip?

---

## 6. Technical Abilities (5 min)

*Goal: Gauge their technical background and how they think about product.*

- What's your background — are you studying computer science, design, or something else?
- Have you built any apps or worked on a software project before? Tell me about it.
- When you find a bug in an app, what do you usually think caused it?
- If you were testing Bloggo, how would you go about finding problems?

> **If relevant:** We use on-device AI — the photos never leave your phone. Does that kind of technology interest you? Have you worked with anything like that?

---

## 7. English Communication & Cultural Fit (5 min)

*Goal: Assess comfort with English in a startup environment.*

- How do you usually practice or improve your English?
- What's the hardest part about working in English for you?
- What do you think is the biggest difference between Korean and Silicon Valley work culture?
- How do you usually communicate when you disagree with a teammate's idea?

---

## 8. Motivation & Chemistry (5 min)

*Goal: Are they excited? Do they fit the energy?*

- What made you want to try Bloggo specifically?
- Bloggo is about turning forgotten travel photos into real stories — does that idea excite you personally? Why?
- What kind of "growth moment" are you hoping to get from this program?
- What does good team chemistry look like to you in a startup setting?
- If you could learn one skill from someone building apps in Silicon Valley, what would it be?

---

## Closing (1–2 min)

- Is there anything about Bloggo you wanted to mention that we didn't cover?
- Do you have any questions for me about the app or the program?
