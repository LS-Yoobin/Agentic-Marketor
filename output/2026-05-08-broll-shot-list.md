## THE FRAMEWORK (keep this pinned)

For every scene:
- **3 angles minimum:** Wide (full body + room) → Medium (waist up) → Tight (hands/face/object)
- **Break the action:** 3–5 steps, film each step separately before moving on
- **Mix shot types:** Overhead · POV · Low-angle · Side · 3/4
- **Stills:** After setup is done — 60s self-timer, get 3 stills from same angle

## SHOT TYPE CHEAT SHEET

| Shot | How to Set Up |
|------|--------------|
| Wide | Camera back — full body + environment visible |
| Medium | Waist up, natural conversation framing |
| Tight | Face, hands, or one object fills frame |
| Overhead | Camera directly above — tripod arm, stack of books, or shelf |
| POV | Camera at eye level, facing what you'd see |
| Low angle | Camera below eye level, pointing up |
| 3/4 | 45° from front — most flattering, most natural for talking head |
