## Content Trend Signals

1. **Authentic storytelling beats polish in 2026** — Brands that feel human outperform cookie-cutter content. Specific founder moments (not personas) are the currency. One raw observation > ten polished statements.

2. **Social search is now primary discovery** — TikTok and Instagram are being used as search engines. Content optimized for intent-based search ("travel app that doesn't upload photos") has compounding ROI vs feed-only posts.

3. **Slow travel content dominates travel niche** — "15 countries in 30 days" is dead. Creators showing months in one destination are outperforming. Bloggo's place-level storytelling is perfectly aligned with this shift.

5. **Founder credibility > brand credibility in 2026** — People trust founders with faces over brand accounts. Yoobin posting from a personal account about the Bloggo journey will outperform anything posted from the @Bloggo brand account.

8. **"Proof over promise" is the highest-converting content format** — Screen recordings, demo clips, and before/after comparisons outperform explanation posts. For Bloggo: show the camera roll → blog transformation, not a features list.
