## Content Trend Signals

1. **Authenticity gap opening** — Feeds are saturated with synthetic-looking content. Founders showing real decisions, real moments, real product work are breaking through. Human > polished.

2. **Serialized build-in-public** — One-off posts aren't building audiences. Episode-style content ("Week 3 of building X") compounds over time. Give people a reason to return.

3. **Save > Like** — Instagram algorithm heavily weights saves. Content that teaches something, reframes something, or solves something earns saves. Vanity reach is noise.

4. **1.7-second Reel retention hook** — Short-form hooks matter more than ever. The first second determines if the algorithm pushes content. Travel hook + emotional payoff = ideal combo.

5. **Video demos outperform threads** — A 60-second product demo communicates more than 3 carousels. If you haven't done a screen-record demo of Bloggo's "Tap to Blog" flow, that's the gap.

6. **Personality carousels trending** — "Some people call me ___" format, "debate-style" posts. Works when it reveals something true about your category (not personal trivia).

7. **Building in public: transparency over perfection** — Founders who share failed experiments, wrong turns, and real costs are outperforming polished launch announcements.

8. **Travel content: context > destinations** — What made a place *feel* a certain way beats landmark content. Mood, memory, and story are resonating more than "best places in X."
