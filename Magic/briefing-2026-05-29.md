## Content Trend Signals

1. **Authenticity > polish — unfiltered content dominates May 2026** — Behind-the-scenes reality, honest process, and visible imperfection outperform curated brand content. Audiences have developed "polish blindness." Raw moment content earns 2-3× more comments and saves.

2. **AI-assisted content + clear human POV is the winning formula** — AI production alone generates ignored generic filler. The combination of AI speed + founder judgment = trust + reach. Position Yoobin explicitly as the judgment layer on top of AI tools.

3. **Watch time is Instagram's #1 algorithm signal in May 2026** — Completion rate now outranks follower count and like count for reach. Carousel swipe-through rate and Reel watch time are the metrics to optimize. Hooks that make people finish matter more than ever.

4. **Travel Reels: urgency hooks hitting 100K–268K views** — Format "I literally leave for [COUNTRY] in 2 HOURS" consistently earns outsized reach. Temporal urgency + travel combination is the top-performing travel hook format right now. Bloggo can adapt this with "Your memories are fading right now" framing.

5. **Building in public: process > metrics** — Founders showing their actual workflow (tool stack, decisions made, things that broke) outperform those posting revenue screenshots. The "how I made this" format earns more saves than the "look at my results" format.

6. **Founder face-time premium** — Founders who appear on camera get outsized trust and engagement vs. brand-voice posts. Especially on Reels. Yoobin showing his own use of Bloggo and his agent workspace is high-leverage content.

7. **Social search behavior: platforms are now parallel discovery engines** — Gen Z and Millennials search Instagram and TikTok before Google for app recommendations. "Best travel blog app iOS" type queries are happening on Instagram. ASO and social discoverability should be treated as one strategy.

8. **Niche communities > mass broadcast** — Micro-communities (travel photographers, solo founders, memory keepers, iOS power users) convert better than broad reach. Focus Bloggo's content distribution on serving specific niches deeply.
