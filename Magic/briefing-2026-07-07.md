## Content Trend Signals

1. **DM shares 3–5× more valuable than likes.** Instagram's algorithm in 2026 weights DM shares 3–5× higher than likes for Reels distribution. → Design every carousel to be *share-worthy*, not just likeable. Ask: "Would someone DM this to a friend who needs to hear it?"

2. **Social platforms are the new Google.** Gen Z uses TikTok and Instagram as search engines — searching "best travel blog app", "how to make a travel blog", "how to start a travel journal." → Optimize captions with keyword-rich copy, not hashtag spam.

3. **Completion rate > everything.** Instagram measures completion rate above almost everything else — if someone swipes away early, the post is buried. → Hook slide must work in 0.3 seconds. The "6,000 photos" hook format is built for this.

4. **Travel creators are dominating with cinematic b-roll + text overlays.** The winning format: aspirational location footage + text revealing "5 things no one told me" or "what actually happened on this trip." → Bloggo's "turn your camera roll into a story" message maps directly onto this trend.

5. **Glitch edits + "4 emotional tones" format going viral on TikTok.** Creators deliver one line in 4 emotional registers (happy, angry, sarcastic, flirty). → Yoobin can adapt: "The trip I almost didn't document" in 4 emotional tones.

6. **AI + human hybrid wins.** Brands using AI for drafts + human voice for editing outperform both fully-AI and fully-manual content. → Bloggo carousels should be template-generated (AI) but caption voice must be Yoobin's own.

7. **Founder-led accounts beat brand accounts algorithmically.** Platform algorithms favor personal accounts over brand pages. → Keep posting from @yoobin first; Bloggo brand account is secondary distribution.

8. **Short-form video sweet spot: 30–90 seconds.** Educational/storytelling Reels in the 30–90 second range outperform both shorter clips and long-form for founder content. → Target this window for any Reel version of the carousel concepts.
