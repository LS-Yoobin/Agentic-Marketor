## Content Trend Signals

1. **Authentic > polished** — Founders who show real work, failures, and unfiltered moments are gaining ground over produced content. The bar for "authentic" keeps rising.

2. **Serialized build-in-public sequences** — Multi-part series (e.g. "Week 3 of building X") are outperforming one-off updates. The ongoing narrative keeps followers engaged across posts.

3. **"How it works" carousels are saving-worthy** — Product education slides that reveal mechanism (not just feature) are getting high save rates. "Your camera roll has GPS — here's what Bloggo does with it" is exactly this format.

4. **TikTok as discovery, Instagram as trust** — The funnel is: see it on TikTok → look up on Instagram → follow if the grid feels credible. Having both presences matters more than ever.

5. **Privacy is a content angle now** — Users are sharing posts about apps that upload their photos to the cloud. "My photos never leave my phone" is a hook that travels.

6. **Short-form product demos with no voiceover** — Screen recordings with captions and ambient audio are outperforming talking-head demos for app content. Consider Bloggo screen captures in Reels.

7. **Founder face + product** — The combination of a real person's face with a product demo is getting the highest comment rates. Yoobin showing Bloggo working on real trips = high engagement content.

8. **Saturation signal on generic "I built an app" posts** — Generic milestone posts ("we hit 100 users!") are underperforming. Specific, behind-the-scenes mechanics are winning instead.
