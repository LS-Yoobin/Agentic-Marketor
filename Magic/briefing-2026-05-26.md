3. **"AI that doesn't spy on you" is a genuine content category now** — Consumer-level concern about ambient AI (Google's Gemini Spark being always-on) is creating an audience that actively seeks alternatives. Privacy-first product education earns strong save rates.

4. **Authenticity pivot is accelerating: spontaneous > polished** — Audiences fatigue on over-produced content quickly in 2026. Raw founder POV content — "here's what I saw this week in my dashboard" — outperforms produced carousels for engagement (though not saves). Mix both.

5. **Product education carousels earn saves, not just likes** — "How it works" content is the highest save-rate format on Instagram in May 2026. Tuesday drops of product education (like today's batch) align perfectly with peak-save-day behavior (Tue/Wed are the highest save days per platform data).

6. **Short-form demo > long-form explanation for conversion** — A 15-second Reel showing "tap to blog → finished blog in 45 seconds" converts better than any written explanation. The demo is the marketing. Bloggo should be recording screen demos constantly.

7. **Community content > public feed for trust and deals** — Public posts attract attention; private communities convert. Consider a small Bloggo beta Discord or close-friends Instagram list to build the core 100 users who become word-of-mouth engines.

8. **"Building what I wish existed" framing wins with indie founders** — The most shared build-in-public content in May 2026 frames the founder as a frustrated user who built the solution. Yoobin's origin story (photographer who couldn't get travel blogs done) is perfectly shaped for this frame.
