6. **World Cup travel content is wide open** — Billions watching the World Cup means "travel to watch the game" content is highly discoverable right now. Bloggo + World Cup travel stories = a seasonal angle that hasn't been mined yet.

7. **Private micro-communities drive retention** — Public content attracts; private community retains. Bloggo beta community needs a Discord or Slack home, not just a bio link. This is the conversion layer between carousel viewer → loyal early user.

8. **Founder metrics transparency is the #1 trust signal** — Sharing real numbers (downloads, revenue, rejections) is the highest-performing building-in-public content format in August 2026. Even "Week 1: 0 downloads" is compelling. Yoobin sharing real Bloggo launch metrics will compound.
