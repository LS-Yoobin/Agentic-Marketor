## Content Trend Signals

1. **Social search has replaced Google for Gen Z travelers.** 65% of Gen Z now use TikTok as their primary trip-planning tool. #TravelTok has 250B+ views and accelerating. → Optimize all Bloggo and Yoobin content with search-friendly captions ("travel blog app", "how to organize trip photos").

2. **Reels for discovery, carousels for trust — this is now the official two-format strategy.** Reels get new eyes on you; carousels convert those eyes into followers who save and act. → Keep shipping daily carousels; pair with one weekly Reel showing Bloggo in action.

3. **"Lived experience" beats polished ads.** Audiences want proof of personal journey — demos, build logs, real use cases. Anything that looks like a brand ad gets ignored. → Yoobin posting his own Bloggo use (real trips, real camera roll) outperforms any copy-only carousel.

4. **Small creator trust > celebrity reach.** Audience fit and demonstrated authenticity matter more than follower count. A 5K engaged following outperforms a 500K cold one for a niche app launch. → 1,000 true fans is a real launch strategy. Focus on depth.

5. **Sunday aspirational content outperforms.** Trip inspiration, memory-making moments, and "imagine you were here" posts peak in saves and shares on Sunday. → Today's carousels are perfectly timed.

6. **Creator-led brand accounts getting 3x the organic push.** Platforms reward personal accounts over brand accounts algorithmically. → Yoobin's personal account should always post first; Bloggo account reposts or posts a variation 24h later.

7. **The "build-in-public" category is expanding fast.** Indie hackers sharing raw product decisions, customer conversations, and honest setbacks are pulling massive TikTok and Instagram Reels reach. → One raw "this is what I'm building and why" Reel per week is worth 10 polished carousels.

8. **Destination travel content from Southeast Asia is exploding.** Laos, rural Philippines, Cambodia (off-beat) are pulling record engagement from creators showing untouched sides of regions. → A "Bloggo a Southeast Asia trip" demo would tap directly into this trend.
