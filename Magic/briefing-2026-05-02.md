1. **Founder-led content beats brand content in 2026** — Operators posting clear opinions with real proof outperform polished brand handles. People trust humans before logos. Yoobin's account > Bloggo's account for early-stage growth.

3. **Micro-drama and nostalgic edits dominate short-form** — "6 months later I finally looked at those photos" style micro-drama performs well on both TikTok and Reels. Bloggo's pain point of forgotten trips is perfectly timed.

4. **AI tool-use content surging on LinkedIn and TikTok** — Founders showing their AI workflows (especially agents, Claude, Cursor) get outsized engagement. The workflow, not just the output, is the content.

5. **Privacy anxiety is mainstream, not niche** — Big Tech capex spend coverage has re-surfaced privacy concerns for general audiences. "Your data stays on your phone" is a headline that lands beyond tech users now.

6. **Chaos culture + cozy slow-living as twin content poles** — The two content aesthetics that dominate 2026: chaotic/funny reality vs. slow, quiet, intentional living. Bloggo lives naturally in the slow-living pole — "remember the trip properly."

7. **Social search has replaced Google for Gen Z** — TikTok and Instagram are now the first search for travel destinations, app discovery, and product research. Optimize carousel copy for search-discoverability, not just scroll-stopping.

8. **Sustainability + intentionality messaging resonates in travel content** — Post-conflict travel sentiment is shifting toward meaning > volume. "This trip deserved a story" framing maps perfectly to current mood.
