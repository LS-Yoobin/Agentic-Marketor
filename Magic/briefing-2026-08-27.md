## Content Trend Signals

1. **Authenticity > production.** Single-take founder clips, unedited POV, "messy middle" content — outperforming high-production brand content on both TikTok and Instagram Reels. Post raw. The carousel workflow already optimizes for this.

2. **Social platforms are search engines.** Captions and spoken words are indexed. "Best travel blog app," "AI travel journal," and "how to make a travel blog" are live search terms — not just hashtags. Keyword-rich captions are now an ASO-equivalent strategy for social.

3. **Community > reach.** Saves, DM shares, and comment replies are weighted over passive views. Every carousel CTA should earn saves, not hearts. Design for the bookmark.

4. **Building-in-public is peaking.** Founders showing failed experiments in 45 seconds, employees showing real product usage — these formats are getting outsized organic reach on TikTok Aug 2026. Yoobin should be posting the Bloggo build journey weekly.

5. **Carousel series > one-off posts.** Consistent visual identity in a series builds 4x more long-term reach than standalone posts. The daily carousel workflow is the right call.

6. **People trust founders more than logos.** Post from Yoobin's personal account first. The Bloggo brand account is secondary until launch momentum builds.

7. **Long-tail ASO keywords rising.** "AI travel journal" and "travel blog from photos" have rising App Store search volume with low competition. File these now.

8. **Short-form sweet spot: 30–90 seconds for education.** Reels and TikToks in this range are outperforming both sub-15s and longer formats for founder/product education content.
