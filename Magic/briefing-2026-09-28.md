Top BIP (build-in-public) creators on X — Pieter Levels, Marc Lou, Tony Dinh — win because they distribute consistently, not because their product is better. *Signal: weekly Bloggo progress posts on X/Instagram beats occasional big announcements.*

Show the trip output, not the app UI.*

5. **Short-form hooks under 3 seconds are everything.** The first 3 seconds of a Reel now determines 80% of reach. Strong text overlay in the first frame is the single highest-leverage thing a creator can test. *Signal: every Bloggo Reel needs a text hook visible before the audio plays — assume muted viewers.*

6. **AI tool demos are getting scroll-fatigue on social.** The "watch AI do X" format is oversaturated. Content that shows the emotional outcome (the story, the memory, the moment) beats content that shows the technical process. *Signal: "Here's my Japan blog" beats "Watch Bloggo generate a blog in 60 seconds."*

7. **Creator-led trust > brand trust.** Founder faces on camera convert significantly better than brand accounts. Yoobin posting from the founder account will outperform the Bloggo brand account for awareness. *Signal: prioritize personal account content over Bloggo brand account for top-of-funnel.*

8. **Sunday is the highest-engagement day for aspirational/travel content.** Travel saves and shares peak on Sunday evenings when people are planning or dreaming. *Signal: posting today's Sunday aspirational carousel now is well-timed.*
