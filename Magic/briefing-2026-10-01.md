## Content Trend Signals

1. **DM shares > likes.** Content people send privately now carries more commercial weight than public likes. Every carousel should earn a DM share — design for "I need to send this to someone." The Thursday relatable angle is perfect for this.

2. **Search-driven social is non-negotiable.** TikTok and Instagram are being used as search engines. Keywords like "travel blog app", "how to document a trip", and "camera roll organize" are search terms — not just hashtags. Optimize captions with these exact phrases.

3. **"Some people call me…" trend is breaking for small businesses.** Small founders listing their roles and ending with their product pitch is dominating TikTok. Yoobin should adapt this format for a 30-second Reel.

4. **"Rock, paper, scissors" debate format is viral.** The format pits two habits against each other — "write a travel blog" (loses) vs. "let the photos sit forever" (also loses) → "let Bloggo write it" (wins). Easy adaptation for a short Reel.

5. **Building in public content on TikTok needs clarity.** Content that attracts only founders, not potential users, has a key flaw: each video must make sense to someone who has never seen you before. Yoobin's build-in-public posts need to lead with the user's pain, not the founder's journey.

6. **Human-led, proof-heavy content wins.** Real demos, real screenshots, and real results outperform polished AI-generated visuals. A screen recording of "tap to blog" working in 60 seconds is worth 10 carousels.

7. **Saves are the new metric.** Instagram's ranking weights saves heavily. Every carousel should have a save-bait slide (Slide 9 in the 8-slide format). Today's "The problem was never the writing. It was the starting." is strong save-bait.

8. **October founder content calendar:** Disrupt 2026 (Oct 13–15) is the week to amplify build-in-public content. Schedule your most personal founder post for Oct 13 to ride the founder audience surge.
