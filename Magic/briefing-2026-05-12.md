## Content Trend Signals

1. **Raw > polished.** Instagram and TikTok algorithms are punishing high-production content and rewarding authenticity. Unedited founder POV clips are outperforming studio shoots. → Post more "messy middle" content.

2. **Series > one-offs.** Carousel series with a consistent visual identity build 4x more long-term reach than standalone posts. → The daily carousel workflow is the right move.

3. **Indie hackers are taking over TikTok.** Founders sharing raw struggles, product decisions, and behind-the-scenes build processes are getting massive organic reach. → Yoobin should be posting the Bloggo build journey weekly.

4. **Saves > likes.** Instagram's ranking now weights saves, DM shares, and watch time — not likes. → Every carousel should end with "save this" — design content to earn saves, not hearts.

5. **Social = search engine.** Gen Z uses TikTok and Instagram as search engines. "Best travel blog app" and "how to make a travel blog" are search terms — not just hashtags. → Optimize video captions with keyword-rich copy.

6. **Founder-led brand beats corporate messaging.** Platforms now algorithmically favor personal accounts over brand accounts. → Post from Yoobin's personal account first, Bloggo's account second.

7. **Privacy content is breaking through.** Privacy-angle content ("your data isn't safe" → "here's how to protect it") is getting extremely high saves and shares — especially on LinkedIn and Instagram. → The Saturday privacy angle carousel is high-potential every week.

8. **Short-form sweet spot: 30–90 seconds for education.** Educational/storytelling Reels that are 30–90s are outperforming both sub-15s clips and longer formats for founder content.
