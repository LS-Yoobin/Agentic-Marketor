## Content Trend Signals

1. **Authenticity > aesthetics — September 2026 belongs to founders who look real.** AI has flooded feeds with generic polished content; human and imperfect content now wins. Raw founder clips outperform studio shoots. → Post more "messy middle" moments.

2. **Employee-Generated Content (EGC) is the breakout trend of 2026.** Brands that activate their team members (or founder) as creators are dramatically outperforming faceless brand accounts. → Yoobin's personal account should be the primary growth channel, not the Bloggo brand account.

3. **Comments are becoming content.** Posts designed to trigger disagreement, debate, or "wait, really?" reactions travel further than posts optimized for likes. → WTF-hook carousels (like today's) are the right direction.

4. **September = "back to building" season.** After summer slowdown, founders and builders are returning to routines, starting projects, and looking for tools. This is high-intent discovery season for productivity and creative apps. → Bloggo launch timing is well-matched.

5. **Saveable carousels + searchable captions are the winning combo.** Instagram's algorithm weights saves + DM shares heavily. Gen Z also uses Instagram as a search engine. → Design every carousel to earn a save, and write keyword-rich captions.

6. **TikTok brands now behave like creators, not advertisers.** The standout brands in September are acting like community members — deadpan reveals, underdog origin stories, unscripted reactions. → Bloggo's tone (warm, delightful, confident) is a perfect fit.

7. **Reels sweet spot is 30–90 seconds for educational/storytelling content.** Under 15s is too quick to build trust; over 2 min loses mobile attention. → Plan short educational Reels around Bloggo's core insight ("your camera roll has your travel journal").

8. **Travel content is high-interest in September.** People returning from summer travel are sitting on camera rolls full of unused photos — exactly the Bloggo pain point. The next 6 weeks are the highest-relevance window of the year.
