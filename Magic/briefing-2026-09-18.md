7. **TikTok Hook Must Work in the First Second** — Educational/professional content performs well only when the first second delivers a clear reason to stay. Lead with the outcome or tension, not the intro.

8. **7–15 Second Reels With a Strong First-Second Hook Are the Viral Formula** — Most viewer drop-off happens in the first 3–5 seconds. For product demos, show the output (the generated blog) before showing the process.
